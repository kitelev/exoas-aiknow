**THIRD-status generalization: one reusable "Is terminal" precondition applied

in BOTH directions (positive gate + negated gate)** (child `trashed-terminal`, req `127763f8`

generalization** (Done → {Done, Trashed} terminal):
- **(a) Reusable atomic used in BOTH directions makes it non-vacuous by construction.** A single

  (ii) **negatively** — its `NotPrecondition` («Visible until terminal») is the drop-in successor to the

  directions guarantees CQ3 non-vacuity (leaf in ≥2 composites) AND keeps the terminal definition
  single-sourced (add a 3rd terminal status → edit one asset). Prefer this over duplicating the
